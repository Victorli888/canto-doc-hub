## Phrases

1. **我係軟件工程師。**
   ngo5 hai6 jyun5 gin6 gung1 cing4 si1.
   *I'm a software engineer.*

2. **我喺一間做預製石屎嘅公司返工。**
   ngo5 hai2 jat1 gaan1 zou6 jyu6 zai3 sek6 si2 ge3 gung1 si1 faan1 gung1.
   *I work at a precast concrete company.*

3. **我負責公司嘅網上商店平台。**
   ngo5 fu6 zaak3 gung1 si1 ge3 mong5 soeng6 soeng1 dim3 ping4 toi4.
   *I'm responsible for the company's e-commerce platform.*

4. **我開發同維護個電子商務系統。**
   ngo5 hoi1 faat3 tung4 wai4 wu6 go3 din6 zi2 soeng1 mou6 hai6 tung2.
   *I develop and maintain the e-commerce system.*

5. **我哋公司整石屎基建產品。**
   ngo5 dei6 gung1 si1 zing2 sek6 si2 gei1 gin3 caan2 ban2.
   *Our company makes concrete infrastructure products.*

6. **例如沙井、渠管同擋土牆。**
   lai6 jyu4 saa1 zeng2, keoi4 gun2 tung4 dong2 tou2 coeng4.
   *For example, manholes, drainage pipes and retaining walls.*

7. **啲產品喺工廠預先整好，再運去工地。**
   di1 caan2 ban2 hai2 gung1 cong2 jyu6 sin1 zing2 hou2, zoi3 wan6 heoi3 gung1 dei6.
   *The products are made in the factory ahead of time, then shipped to the construction site.*

8. **承建商可以喺網上落單。**
   sing4 gin3 soeng1 ho2 ji5 hai2 mong5 soeng6 lok6 daan1.
   *Contractors can place orders online.*

9. **我寫程式㗎，唔使倒石屎！**
   ngo5 se2 cing4 sik1 gaa3, m4 sai2 dou3 sek6 si2!
   *I write code — I don't have to pour concrete!* (good joke when people assume you work on site)

10. **我啱啱開始呢份工。**
    ngo5 ngaam1 ngaam1 hoi1 ci2 nei1 fan6 gung1.
    *I just started this job.*

## Notes

- **石屎 vs 混凝土:** In conversation, 石屎 (sek6 si2) is what you'll hear in Hong Kong. 混凝土 (wan6 jing4 tou2) is the textbook/written term.
- **網上商店 vs 電子商務:** 網上商店 sounds more natural in casual talk; 電子商務 is fine in a work/technical context.
- **整 vs 生產:** 整 (zing2) is casual "make"; 生產 (saang1 caan2) is "manufacture," more formal.
