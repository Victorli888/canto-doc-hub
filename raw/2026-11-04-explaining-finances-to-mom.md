# Cantonese: Explaining Finances to Mom (Two Separate Banks)

Dumped by Victor 2026-11-04. Immutable. **One Jyutping error uncorrected here:
呀嘛 is aa6 maa5, not aa3 maa3.** See [[explaining-finances-to-mom-dump]] for the
verified version; use the wiki pages, not this file.

**Goal:** Explain to Mom, warmly and simply, that her money is split between two banks, that the banks are completely separate, and that one bank can't see what's in the other. Also explain what a high-yield savings account and a Roth IRA are.

**Register:** Family and casual. Use 媽 (maa1) and short, everyday sentences.

## Phrases (in conversation order)

1. **媽，我同你解釋下你啲錢擺咗喺邊。**
   maa1, ngo5 tung4 nei5 gaai2 sik1 haa5 nei5 di1 cin2 baai2 zo2 hai2 bin1.
   *Mom, let me explain where your money is.*

2. **你啲錢分開咗擺喺兩間銀行。**
   nei5 di1 cin2 fan1 hoi1 zo2 baai2 hai2 loeng5 gaan1 ngan4 hong4.
   *Your money is split between two banks.*

3. **呢兩間銀行完全冇關係㗎。**
   nei1 loeng5 gaan1 ngan4 hong4 jyun4 cyun4 mou5 gwaan1 hai6 gaa3.
   *These two banks have nothing to do with each other.*

4. **好似兩個銀包咁，一個唔會知道另一個有幾多錢。**
   hou2 ci5 loeng5 go3 ngan4 baau1 gam2, jat1 go3 m4 wui5 zi1 dou3 ling6 jat1 go3 jau5 gei2 do1 cin2.
   *It's like two wallets: one doesn't know how much is in the other.*

5. **所以你喺呢間睇唔到嗰間嘅結餘。**
   so2 ji5 nei5 hai2 nei1 gaan1 tai2 m4 dou2 go2 gaan1 ge3 git3 jyu4.
   *So at this bank, you can't see the balance at that bank.*

6. **呢個係高息儲蓄戶口，利息高過普通戶口。**
   nei1 go3 hai6 gou1 sik1 cyu5 cuk1 wu6 hau2, lei6 sik1 gou1 gwo3 pou2 tung1 wu6 hau2.
   *This is a high-yield savings account. The interest is higher than a regular account.*

7. **啲錢隨時都可以攞出嚟。**
   di1 cin2 ceoi4 si4 dou1 ho2 ji5 lo2 ceot1 lai4.
   *You can take the money out anytime.*

8. **Roth IRA 係退休戶口。**
   Roth IRA hai6 teoi3 jau1 wu6 hau2.
   *A Roth IRA is a retirement account.*

9. **放入去嘅錢已經交咗稅，退休攞出嚟就唔使再交稅。**
   fong3 jap6 heoi3 ge3 cin2 ji5 ging1 gaau1 zo2 seoi3, teoi3 jau1 lo2 ceot1 lai4 zau6 m4 sai2 zoi3 gaau1 seoi3.
   *The money that goes in has already been taxed, so when you take it out in retirement you don't pay tax again.*

10. **不過賺返嚟嘅錢，未夠年紀攞出嚟可能要罰錢。**
    bat1 gwo3 zaan6 faan1 lai4 ge3 cin2, mei6 gau3 nin4 gei2 lo2 ceot1 lai4 ho2 nang4 jiu3 fat6 cin2.
    *But if you take out the earnings before you're old enough, there may be a penalty.*

11. **銀行啲錢有政府保障，唔使擔心。**
    ngan4 hong4 di1 cin2 jau5 zing3 fu2 bou2 zoeng3, m4 sai2 daam1 sam1.
    *The money in the banks is protected by the government, so don't worry.*

12. **如果要轉錢，我可以幫你轉賬。**
    jyu4 gwo2 jiu3 zyun2 cin2, ngo5 ho2 ji5 bong1 nei5 zyun2 zoeng3.
    *If you need to move money, I can do the transfer for you.*

13. **唔好俾人知你個密碼呀。**
    m4 hou2 bei2 jan4 zi1 nei5 go3 mat6 maa5 aa3.
    *Don't let anyone know your password.*

## Mini Dialogue (practice with Mom's likely questions)

**媽：點解呢間銀行話我得咁少錢嘅？**
dim2 gaai2 nei1 gaan1 ngan4 hong4 waa6 ngo5 dak1 gam3 siu2 cin2 ge2?
*Why does this bank say I only have this little money?*

**你：因為佢只係睇到佢自己嗰份，另一間嘅錢佢睇唔到。**
jan1 wai6 keoi5 zi2 hai6 tai2 dou2 keoi5 zi6 gei2 go2 fan6, ling6 jat1 gaan1 ge3 cin2 keoi5 tai2 m4 dou2.
*Because it only sees its own part. It can't see the money at the other bank.*

**媽：咁我啲錢冇唔見呀嘛？**
gam2 ngo5 di1 cin2 mou5 m4 gin3 aa3 maa3.
*So my money isn't lost, right?*

**你：冇唔見，全部都喺度，只係分開咗擺。**
mou5 m4 gin3, cyun4 bou6 dou1 hai2 dou6, zi2 hai6 fan1 hoi1 zo2 baai2.
*Nothing's lost. It's all there, just kept in separate places.*

## Practice

1. Say phrase 4 (the two-wallets analogy) from memory. This one line usually gets the whole idea across.
2. Swap 銀行 for 戶口 in phrase 2 to say "your money is in two accounts": 你啲錢分開咗擺喺兩個戶口 (note the measure word changes from 間 to 個).
3. Explain the Roth IRA in one breath by combining phrases 8 and 9.

## Notes

- **戶口 vs 帳戶:** In HK Cantonese, an account is 戶口. 帳戶 is the Mandarin/written word.
- **攞錢 / 放錢 vs 提款 / 存款:** 攞錢 and 放錢入 are what you'd say to family. 提款 (tai4 fun2) and 存款 (cyun4 fun2) are the formal banking terms you'd see on an ATM or form.
- **Roth IRA:** No everyday Cantonese term exists, so say "Roth IRA" in English and follow up with 退休戶口. Code-switching like this is completely normal in HK speech.
- **Accuracy check:** Government protection (FDIC) covers money in the bank accounts, not investments inside a Roth IRA. That's why phrase 11 says 銀行啲錢 (the money in the banks). Also, Roth contributions can usually be taken out anytime; the early-withdrawal penalty applies mainly to the earnings, which is why phrase 10 says 賺返嚟嘅錢.
- **Measure words:** 一間銀行 (a bank), 一個戶口 (an account), 一個銀包 (a wallet).
