「唔係呀，唔係為咗賺錢」 is grammatically correct in Hong Kong Cantonese, but 「為咗賺錢」 can sound slightly deliberate or explanatory in casual conversation.

A Hong Kong local might express it more naturally in the following ways:
Cantonese	Meaning / Nuance
唔係呀，唔係諗住賺錢㗎。	No, I'm not doing it with the intention of making money.
唔係呀，我又唔係想賺錢。	No, it's not like I'm trying to make money.
唔係呀，我又唔係靠呢樣嘢搵食。	No, it's not like I make a living doing this.
唔係呀，純粹係興趣啫。	No, it's purely a hobby.
唔係呀，冇諗住靠呢樣嘢賺錢。	No, I'm not planning to make money from this.
唔係呀，玩吓啫。	No, I'm just doing it for fun.
