# What Works

The synthesis page for learning strategies. **Deliberately almost empty** — its content
has to come from Victor's own evidence, not from generic advice. Generic advice about
language learning is free and worthless; what's valuable is the record of what he tried,
what stuck, and what quietly stopped happening after two weeks.

Strategies with enough to say get their own page in `wiki/strategies/`. This page holds
the verdicts.

## Open questions

Things worth finding out, to be answered by experience rather than looked up:

- Does capturing a phrase the same day it's heard make it stick better than capturing it
  in a weekly batch?
- Which `new` phrases make it to `active`, and what do they have in common? (Check
  `review.md` once there's a month of data — this is the question that tells us what
  kind of phrase is worth capturing at all.)
- Is passive listening (TV, podcasts) moving production, or only comprehension?

## How to use this page

When a strategy gets tried, the LLM adds a row with the date and an honest verdict —
including "abandoned", which is the most useful verdict and the one most likely to go
unrecorded. Abandoning something is data.
