# Softening and Politeness

Cantonese politeness runs through particles, verb-doubling and framing far more than
through a "please" word. 請 (cing2) exists but is written and formal — using it in casual
speech is one of the clearest textbook tells.

Three mechanisms do most of the work:

1. **A final particle** — 吖, 啦. See [[final-particles]].
2. **Doubling or 吓 (haa5)** — 睇吓 "have a quick look", 傾吓 "have a bit of a chat". It
   makes the request small, and small requests are easy to grant.
3. **A tag question** — 好唔好? 得唔得? Turning a statement into a choice.

## Apologizing and excusing

### 唔好意思 — excuse me / sorry
- **Jyutping:** m4 hou2 ji3 si1
- **Literal:** not-good-meaning
- **Use:** The everyday one. Getting someone's attention, squeezing past, a small
  apology, declining something. Vastly more common than a real apology.
- **Register:** neutral, works everywhere.
- **Contrast:** 對唔住 is for things that actually warrant sorry. Using it for bumping
  someone on the MTR sounds heavy.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-01 · source: seed

### 對唔住 — I'm sorry
- **Jyutping:** deoi3 m4 zyu6
- **Use:** A genuine apology for something you did.
- **Register:** neutral to serious.
- **Contrast:** see 唔好意思 above. The two are not interchangeable in either direction.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-01 · source: seed

## Asking for something

### 麻煩你 — if you wouldn't mind
- **Jyutping:** maa4 faan4 nei5
- **Literal:** trouble you
- **Use:** Prefaces a request, or thanks someone for one they're about to do. 「麻煩你」on
  its own, handing something over, is complete.
- **Register:** neutral to polite. Good with shop staff, colleagues, strangers.
- **Contrast:** this is the slot where a learner reaches for 請. Use 麻煩你 instead.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-01 · source: seed

### 幫我…吖 — could you … for me
- **Jyutping:** bong1 ngo5 … aa1
- **Use:** The standard casual request frame. 「幫我開個窗吖」= "get the window for me,
  would you."
- **Register:** casual. Friends, close colleagues.
- **See also:** [[final-particles]]
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-01 · source: seed

## Receiving

### 唔使客氣 — don't mention it
- **Jyutping:** m4 sai2 haak3 hei3
- **Literal:** no-need guest-manner
- **Use:** Response to thanks.
- **Register:** neutral.
- **Contrast:** 唔使客氣 answers thanks; 唔好客氣 (m4 hou2 …) *invites* someone to relax
  and help themselves — "don't be shy." Same shape, different job.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-01 · source: seed

### 唔使 — no need / it's fine
- **Jyutping:** m4 sai2
- **Use:** Declining an offer. On its own, with a small wave, it's a complete and
  perfectly polite refusal.
- **Register:** neutral.
- **Contrast:** 唔好 (m4 hou2) is "don't" — a prohibition. Declining tea with 唔好 is a
  rebuke, not a refusal.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-01 · source: seed
