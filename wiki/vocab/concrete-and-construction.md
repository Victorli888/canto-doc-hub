# Concrete and Construction Vocabulary

Victor's employer makes precast concrete infrastructure products, so this is shop talk he
will hear every day. The organising fact: **the colloquial word and the written word are
different for the most common term on the list**, and in this trade the colloquial one is
not slang — it is what engineers, site staff and the company's own people say.

Readings checked against [words.hk](https://words.hk) and
[Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org). See [[describing-my-job-dump]] for the
corrections made on ingest.

## Concrete itself

| Chinese | Jyutping | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 石屎 | sek6 si2 | concrete | **The spoken word.** Literally "stone shit" — from how poured concrete heaps up. Also written 石矢. |
| 混凝土 | wan6 jing4 tou2 | concrete | Written / technical. Correct on a drawing, textbook in your mouth. |
| 三合土 | saam1 hap6 tou2 | concrete | Older term, still listed as a synonym. |
| 落石屎 | lok6 sek6 si2 | pour concrete | The trade verb phrase: pouring into formwork and waiting for it to set. The verb is 落, not 倒. |
| 石屎森林 | sek6 si2 sam1 lam4 | concrete jungle | Hong Kong's own cliché for itself. Worth knowing because it comes up constantly. |

## Verbs worth separating

| Chinese | Jyutping | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 做 | zou6 | What a company does as a line of business: 我哋公司做石屎產品. The default. |
| 整 | zing2 | Making one specific thing, by hand: 整個蛋糕, 整嘢食. **Not** for a product line. |
| 生產 | sang1 caan2 | Manufacture. Correct but formal — 生 takes the literary **sang1** here, not saang1. |
| 運 | wan6 | Transport, ship. 運去工地 = ship to site. |
| 落 | lok6 | Put down / pour / place: 落石屎, 落單. A workhorse verb. |
