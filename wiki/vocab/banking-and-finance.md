# Banking and Finance Vocabulary

Terms behind [[talking-about-money]]. Every reading below was checked against the local
dataset ([[words-hk-dataset]]); the few gaps are marked and their components verified
individually.

The spine of this page: **the family word and the counter/form word are different for
almost every operation**, and reaching for the formal one with your mother is the same
mistake as 完成 for 搞掂 at work.

## Moving money — family word vs form word

| Casual (to family) | Formal (on an ATM or form) | English |
|---|---|---|
| 放錢入 fong3 cin2 jap6 | 存款 cyun4 fun2 | put money in / deposit |
| 攞錢 lo2 cin2 | 提款 tai4 fun2 | take money out / withdraw |
| 攞出嚟 lo2 ceot1 lai4 | — | take it out |
| 轉錢 zyun2 cin2 | 轉賬 zyun2 zoeng3 | move money / transfer |
| 存錢 cyun4 cin2 | — | save money (into an account) |

words.hk marks 提款 explicitly as 更為正式嘅講法, "the more formal way of saying it".
Both halves of this table are worth knowing: you say the left one to Mom and you read the
right one on the screen.

## Measure words, collected

一**間**銀行 · 一**個**戶口 · 一**個**銀包 · 一**張**結單

The one that actually bites: swapping 銀行 for 戶口 in a sentence and leaving 間 behind.
