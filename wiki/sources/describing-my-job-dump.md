# Source — "Describing My Job" vocabulary dump

Raw file: [`raw/2026-11-04-describing-my-job.md`](../../raw/2026-11-04-describing-my-job.md)

A vocabulary-and-phrase sheet Victor brought in on 2026-11-04, covering his job: software
engineer on the e-commerce platform at a Hong Kong precast concrete company, recently
started. Two term sets (software, concrete/infrastructure) plus ten sentences.

Fanned out to [[describing-my-job]], [[software-and-tech]] and
[[concrete-and-construction]].

## What it got right

The high-value content is the colloquial-vs-written split, and it is right about the one
that matters most: **石屎 (sek6 si2), not 混凝土 (wan6 jing4 tou2)**. Saying 混凝土 out
loud in Hong Kong is a textbook tell, and for Victor it would be a tell at his own
workplace. Same for 基建 over 基礎建設, and 網上商店 over 電子商務 in casual talk.

The engineering nouns all check out: 沙井 saa1 zeng2, 渠管 keoi4 gun2, 擋土牆
dong2 tou2 coeng4, 化糞池 faa3 fan3 ci4, 承建商 sing4 gin3 soeng1, 預製 jyu6 zai3.

## Corrections made on ingest

Five readings in the raw file are wrong. The raw file is left as-is (raw/ is immutable);
the corrected forms are what the wiki pages carry.

| Raw file | Correct | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 軟件 jyun5 gin6 | **jyun5 gin6-2** (surface gin2) | [words.hk 軟件](https://words.hk/zidin/v/?q=軟件) gives jyun5 gin2; [硬件](https://words.hk/zidin/v/?q=硬件) ngaang6 gin2; Wiktionary 軟件 jyun5 gin6-2 |
| 訂單 ding6 daan1 | **deng6 daan1** | [Wiktionary 訂單](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/訂單) lists only deng6 daan1 — the colloquial 訂 is deng6 |
| 預製組件 zou2 gin2 | **zou2 gin6-2** | [Wiktionary 組件](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/組件) — same changed tone as 軟件 |
| 生產 saang1 caan2 | **sang1 caan2** | [Wiktionary 生產](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/生產) — 生 takes the literary sang1 here, like 生活, not the colloquial saang1 of 生意 |
| 倒石屎 dou3 sek6 si2 | **落石屎 lok6 sek6 si2** | 倒 "pour/dump" is dou2, not dou3 ([Wiktionary 倒](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/倒)); and the Hong Kong site term for pouring concrete is 落石屎 ([混凝土工 — HK construction blog](http://rm501.blogspot.com/2020/09/blog-post.html)) |

The 件 → gin2 pattern is the one worth internalising: it recurs across 軟件, 硬件 and
組件, so it is a rule, not three facts.

## Register changes made on ingest

These are judgment calls, not dictionary facts, and are flagged on the entries:

- **寫程式 → 寫code.** 程式 cing4 sik1 is a real Hong Kong word, but a written one — it
  lives in 應用程式 ("app") on a screen, not in a Hong Kong engineer's mouth. The research
  literature on Hong Kong code-mixing calls this *expedient mixing*: English fills in when
  the available Chinese expression "sounds too formal"
  ([Code-switching in Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code-switching_in_Hong_Kong)).
  寫code is the same shape as 做OT and send個email in [[work-small-talk]].
- **整產品 → 做產品.** 整 is for making a specific thing (整個蛋糕). For what a company
  manufactures as a line of business, the casual verb is 做.
- **開發同維護個電子商務系統** — the 個 clashes with the register. 開發 and 維護 are
  formal verbs; 個 is colloquial "the". Pick one level: 「負責開發同維護公司嘅系統」.

## Still open
