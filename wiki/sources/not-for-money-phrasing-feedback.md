# Source — feedback on 「唔係為咗賺錢」

Raw file: [`raw/2026-11-23-not-for-money-phrasing-feedback.md`](../../raw/2026-11-23-not-for-money-phrasing-feedback.md)

Victor pasted this on 2026-11-23. It critiques 「唔係呀，唔係為咗賺錢」, which the wiki
composed on 2026-11-20 for the moment where a friend asks whether the vlogging is meant
to make money. The author isn't stated. It agrees the line is grammatical, but says 為咗賺錢
sounds deliberate and explanatory, and it offers six replacements. **Victor picked
「唔係呀，玩吓啫。」 as the most natural.**

Fanned out to [[filming-my-family]] (the not-for-money block, rewritten around 玩吓啫)
and [[final-particles]] (a new 啫 block).

## Takeaways

- **為咗 is the stiff part.** It explains a purpose, which suits written text or
  a formal explanation. A friend's casual question gets a casual brush-off, not a stated
  purpose. This is the same pattern as 日常 vs 平時: a correct word, but from a register
  one step too high.
- **啫 does the work.** Four of the six alternatives shrink the claim instead of
  explaining it. 啫 (ze1, "just, no big deal"), 又唔係 ("it's not as if") and 冇諗住
  ("not planning to") all play it down. That makes the line sound relaxed instead of
  defensive.
- **玩吓啫 is the shortest and the one Victor chose.** 玩 + 吓 (the "a bit, casually"
  suffix from 傾吓) + 啫. It's three syllables and needs no topic.
- **搵食 (wan2 sik6) means "make a living".** It's a high-frequency colloquial word the
  wiki didn't have yet. 靠呢樣嘢搵食 means "make my living from this".

## The six alternatives, checked

| Line | Verified pieces |
|---|---|
| 唔係諗住賺錢㗎 | 諗住 nam2 zyu6 "intend" ✓, 㗎 ✓ ([[final-particles]]) |
| 我又唔係想賺錢 | 又唔係 as "it's not like" — usage not checked locally |
| 我又唔係靠呢樣嘢搵食 | 靠 kaau3 "rely on" ✓, 搵食 wan2 sik6 ✓ |
| 純粹係興趣啫 | 純粹 ✓, 興趣 hing3 ceoi3 "hobby" ✓, 啫 ze1 ✓ |
| 冇諗住靠呢樣嘢賺錢 | 諗住 ✓, 靠 ✓ |
