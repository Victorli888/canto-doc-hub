# Describing My Job

The answer to 「你做邊行?」(nei5 zou6 bin1 hong4) and 「你做咩嘅?」— which comes up with
every new person, at every dinner, forever. Victor's version has two parts that each
want different handling: the software side, where the natural Cantonese is half English,
and the concrete side, where the natural Cantonese is a colloquial word (石屎) rather
than the textbook one (混凝土).

Terms live in [[software-and-tech]] and [[concrete-and-construction]]. Office register
generally is [[work-small-talk]]. How the offer got negotiated is [[negotiating-my-salary]].

## What I am

### 軟件工程師 — software engineer
- **Jyutping:** jyun5 gin6-2 gung1 cing4 si1
- **Use:** The job title. 「我係軟件工程師」(ngo5 hai6 …) is a complete answer.
- **Register:** neutral. Works everywhere, and unlike most job vocabulary it does not
  have a stiffer and a looser version — this is the word.
- **Contrast:** the tone is the thing to get right. 件 is gin6 on its own, but in
  軟件 / 硬件 / 組件 it surfaces as **gin2** (a changed tone, written gin6-2). Saying
  jyun5 *gin6* is the mistake, and it is audible.
- **Example:** 我係軟件工程師。— ngo5 hai6 jyun5 gin6-2 gung1 cing4 si1.
- **Status:** learning · captured 2026-11-04 · practised 2026-11-12 · practised 2026-11-25 · source: [[describing-my-job-dump]] · verified
  against words.hk and Wiktionary
- **See also:** [[software-and-tech]], [[tone-sandhi]]

### 寫code — write code
- **Jyutping:** se2 code
- **Use:** What you actually do all day. 「我寫code㗎」, 「我日日寫code」.
- **Register:** casual to neutral — standard in a Hong Kong tech office, including when
  talking to non-technical colleagues.
- **Contrast:** 寫程式 (se2 cing4 sik1) is the form a textbook gives you, and 程式 is a
  real Hong Kong word — but a *written* one. It belongs on a screen (應用程式 = "app"),
  not in your mouth. This is the same code-mixing slot as 做OT and send個email in
  [[work-small-talk]]: English fills in precisely because the Chinese option sounds too
  formal. 編程 (pin1 cing4) is the written verb; also not spoken.
- **Status:** learning · captured 2026-11-04 · practised 2026-11-12 · source: [[describing-my-job-dump]] · ⚠️ unverified
  (the word is certain; the claim that it beats 寫程式 in speech is judgment, not a
  dictionary fact)
- **See also:** [[work-small-talk]], [[software-and-tech]], [[everyday-life#做gym — work out]] (same English-wins slot)

### 啱啱開始呢份工 — I just started this job
- **Jyutping:** ngaam1 ngaam1 hoi1 ci2 ni1 fan6 gung1
- **Use:** The pre-emptive excuse, and genuinely useful for the next few months. 啱啱 is
  "just now / only just" and does the work; 呢份工 is "this job" (份 fan6 is the measure
  word for a job).
- **Register:** casual to neutral.
- **Contrast:** 最近 (zeoi3 gan6, "recently") is vaguer and does not buy you the same
  latitude. 啱啱 says *very* recently.
- **Example:** 我啱啱開始呢份工。— ngo5 ngaam1 ngaam1 hoi1 ci2 ni1 fan6 gung1.
- **Status:** learning · captured 2026-11-04 · practised 2026-11-12 · source: [[describing-my-job-dump]]

## Where I work and what I do there

### 喺一間…嘅公司返工 — work at a company that does …
- **Jyutping:** hai2 jat1 gaan1 … ge3 gung1 si1 faan1 gung1
- **Use:** The frame for naming an employer nobody has heard of — you describe what it
  does instead of what it is called. 間 (gaan1) is the measure word for a company.
- **Register:** casual to neutral, universal.
- **Contrast:** 工作 (gung1 zok3) is the written "work". 返工 is the spoken one, and it
  is a verb about *going to* work, so it sits naturally at the end of the clause where
  English would put "I work at".
- **Example:** 我喺一間做預製石屎嘅公司返工。— ngo5 hai2 jat1 gaan1 zou6 jyu6 zai3
  sek6 si2 ge3 gung1 si1 faan1 gung1. "I work at a precast concrete company."
- **Status:** learning · captured 2026-11-04 · practised 2026-11-12 · practised 2026-11-25 · source: [[describing-my-job-dump]]
- **See also:** [[work-small-talk]]

### 負責 — be responsible for / my patch is
- **Jyutping:** fu6 zaak3
- **Use:** The cleanest way to say which part of the company is yours.
  「我負責公司嘅網上商店平台」.
- **Register:** neutral. Slightly official in flavour, which is right for this job —
  it is the word you want in a work introduction, not with friends.
- **Contrast:** with friends you would skip it: 「我搞公司個online shop」. 搞 (gaau2)
  is the casual verb for being the one who handles something — same root as 搞掂 in
  [[work-small-talk]].
- **Example:** 我負責公司嘅網上商店平台。— ngo5 fu6 zaak3 gung1 si1 ge3 mong5 soeng6
  soeng1 dim3 ping4 toi4.
- **Status:** learning · captured 2026-11-04 · practised 2026-11-12 · practised 2026-11-25 · source: [[describing-my-job-dump]]
- **See also:** [[software-and-tech]]

### 我哋公司做… — my company makes …
- **Jyutping:** ngo5 dei6 gung1 si1 zou6 …
- **Use:** What the business does. 「我哋公司做石屎基建產品」.
- **Register:** casual to neutral — 做 is the default and never wrong.
- **Contrast:** three verbs, three jobs. **做** is the business-line verb (做石屎產品).
  **整** (zing2) is making one specific thing with your hands — 整個蛋糕, 整嘢食 — so
  「公司整產品」is a collocation slip, not a grammar error, and it is the kind that
  marks a learner. **生產** (sang1 caan2) is "manufacture", correct but formal: right
  in a company deck, oversized in conversation.
- **Example:** 我哋公司做石屎基建產品，例如沙井、渠管同擋土牆。— ngo5 dei6 gung1 si1
  zou6 sek6 si2 gei1 gin3 caan2 ban2, lai6 jyu4 saa1 zeng2, keoi4 gun2 tung4 dong2
  tou2 coeng4.
- **Status:** learning · captured 2026-11-04 · practised 2026-11-12 · practised 2026-11-25 · source: [[describing-my-job-dump]]
- **See also:** [[concrete-and-construction]]

### 落單 — place an order
- **Jyutping:** lok6 daan1
- **Use:** The verb, in a shop, a restaurant or a B2B platform alike.
  「承建商可以喺網上落單」= "contractors can order online."
- **Register:** casual to neutral, universal. This is the ordinary word, not jargon.
- **Contrast:** the noun is 訂單 **deng6 daan1** — note 訂 is deng6 in speech, not ding6.
  Same 訂 as 訂枱 (deng6 toi2, book a table). 下單 is the Mainland written form; not used
  in Hong Kong speech.
- **Example:** 承建商可以喺網上落單。— sing4 gin3 soeng1 ho2 ji5 hai2 mong5 soeng6
  lok6 daan1.
- **Status:** learning · captured 2026-11-04 · practised 2026-11-12 · source: [[describing-my-job-dump]] · verified
  against Wiktionary
- **See also:** [[software-and-tech]]

## The joke

### 唔使落石屎 — I don't have to pour concrete
- **Jyutping:** m4 sai2 lok6 sek6 si2
- **Use:** For when someone hears "concrete company" and pictures you on site.
  「我寫code㗎，唔使落石屎!」
- **Register:** casual, friendly. Self-deprecating in the right direction — it concedes
  the soft job rather than claiming the hard one.
- **Contrast:** 落石屎 is the Hong Kong site term for pouring concrete into formwork, and
  it is the collocation to know: the verb is 落, not 倒. (倒 does mean "pour" — and it is
  **dou2**, not dou3 — but 落石屎 is the trade phrase.) The particle 㗎 (gaa3) is what
  makes it a correction of an assumption rather than a bare statement; without it the
  line is flat.
- **Status:** learning · captured 2026-11-04 · practised 2026-11-12 · practised 2026-11-25 · source: [[describing-my-job-dump]] · ⚠️ unverified
  (落石屎 and 㗎 are each verified; the assembled joke is composed, so its naturalness is
  not)
- **See also:** [[final-particles]], [[concrete-and-construction]]

## How I actually write code now

### 叫AI寫code — get AI to write the code
- **Jyutping:** giu3 AI se2 code
- **Use:** 叫 is "get someone to do something for you" — the natural verb for handing a
  task to AI, not just using a tool. 「我叫AI幫我寫code」= "I get AI to write the code
  for me."
- **Register:** casual, standard in HK tech talk. Mildly personifies the AI, which is how
  engineers actually talk about it day to day.
- **Contrast:** 用AI寫code (jung6 AI se2 code) is the flatter, more descriptive version —
  "I use AI to write code." Both are fine; 叫 carries a bit more "I delegated this."
- **Status:** learning · wished 2026-11-05 · practised 2026-11-12 · ⚠️ unverified (both words verified individually;
  the pairing is composed)
- **See also:** [[software-and-tech]]

### 我負責判斷，AI負責寫 — I handle the judgment calls, AI does the writing
- **Jyutping:** ngo5 fu6 zaak3 pun3 dyun3, AI fu6 zaak3 se2
- **Use:** The one-line summary of the whole workflow, good for a work introduction or
  explaining what your job actually is now. 判斷 (pun3 dyun3) is "to judge / decide",
  negotiating fine against 做決定.
- **Register:** neutral — carries a hint of structure, right for describing a process to
  a colleague rather than chatting with a friend.
- **Status:** learning · wished 2026-11-05 · practised 2026-11-12 · ⚠️ unverified (composed from verified chunks)
- **See also:** [[software-and-tech]]

### AI寫完，我自己要check同改 — once AI's written it, I check it and fix it myself
- **Jyutping:** AI se2 jyun4, ngo5 zi6 gei2 jiu3 check tung4 goi2
- **Use:** The review step. 改 (goi2) is the ordinary word for fixing/correcting; check is
  the English loanword, same register slot as 寫code.
- **Register:** casual.
- **Contrast:** 校對 (gaau3 deoi3) is attested and means "check against the standard" —
  but it reads as proofreading a document, not reviewing code. 自己check matches this
  wiki's existing pattern (寫code, not 寫程式) better than reaching for the Chinese
  technical word. Judgment call, same shape as that one.
- **Status:** learning · wished 2026-11-05 · practised 2026-11-12 · ⚠️ unverified
- **See also:** [[software-and-tech]]

### 用AI快好多 / 慳好多時間 — using AI is a lot faster / saves a lot of time
- **Jyutping:** jung6 AI faai3 hou2 do1 / haan1 hou2 do1 si4 gaan3
- **Use:** Two short tags for why you do it this way. 慳 (haan1) is "to save" (money,
  time), the everyday word, not a technical one.
- **Register:** casual.
- **Status:** learning · wished 2026-11-05 · practised 2026-11-12
- **See also:** [[software-and-tech]]

### Composite — describing the whole workflow in one go
我依家寫code嘅方法，就係叫AI幫我寫，啲重要嘅判斷同設計我自己做，寫完我再check同改。
ngo5 ji1 gaa1 se2 code ge3 fong1 faat3, zau6 hai6 giu3 AI bong1 ngo5 se2, di1 zung6 jiu3
ge3 pun3 dyun3 tung4 cit3 gai3 ngo5 zi6 gei2 zou6, se2 jyun4 ngo5 zoi3 check tung4 goi2.
*The way I write code now is: I get AI to write it, I handle the important judgment
calls and design myself, and once it's written I check it over and fix it.*
