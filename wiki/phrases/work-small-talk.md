# Work Small Talk

The office register: casual but not slangy, and heavily code-switched with English in
most Hong Kong workplaces. Fighting the English is a mistake — 做OT, send個email, book間
房 are what people actually say, and translating them into pure Cantonese sounds stranger
than the code-switching does.

Saying what your job *is*, rather than getting through the day in it, is
[[describing-my-job]]. Term lists: [[software-and-tech]].

## Getting through the day

### 搞掂 — done / sorted
- **Jyutping:** gaau2 dim6
- **Literal:** handle–settled
- **Use:** A task is finished. 「搞掂喇!」= "Done!" Also "I'll handle it": 「我搞掂佢」.
- **Register:** casual; completely normal at work, not for formal writing.
- **Contrast:** 完成 (jyun4 sing4) is the written/neutral "complete". Saying it out loud
  about a small task is textbook-sounding.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-01 · source: seed
- **See also:** [[final-particles]]

### 收工 — knock off
- **Jyutping:** sau1 gung1
- **Literal:** collect–work
- **Use:** Finish for the day. 「幾點收工?」= "what time do you get off?" The pair is
  返工 (faan1 gung1), to go to / be at work.
- **Register:** casual, universal.
- **Contrast:** 下班 is the Mandarin-flavoured written form — recognizable but not spoken
  in HK.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-01 · source: seed
- **See also:** [[everyday-life#放工仲要…]] (放工 vs 收工)

### 開會 — have a meeting
- **Jyutping:** hoi1 wui6-2
- **Use:** 「開緊會」= "in a meeting right now." The changed tone (wui6 → wui2) is the
  spoken norm.
- **Register:** neutral.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-01 · source: seed · verified against CantoWords and
  Wiktionary
- **See also:** [[lazy-sounds]], [[tone-sandhi]]

### 做OT — work overtime
- **Jyutping:** zou6 OT
- **Use:** Exactly as written — the English initialism inside a Cantonese verb phrase.
  「今晚做OT」= "working late tonight."
- **Register:** casual, standard in HK offices.
- **Contrast:** 加班 is the written/Mandarin form. Nobody says it in a HK office.
  Same slot as 寫code over 寫程式 — see [[describing-my-job]].
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-01 · source: seed
- **See also:** [[software-and-tech]]

## Talking to people

### 傾吓 — have a chat
- **Jyutping:** king1 haa5
- **Use:** 「傾吓啦」= "let's have a chat about it." The 吓 makes it brief and low-stakes,
  which is what gets the meeting accepted.
- **Register:** casual to neutral.
- **Contrast:** 討論 (tou2 leon6) is "discuss" — formal, and oversized for a two-minute
  conversation.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-01 · source: seed
- **See also:** [[softening-and-politeness]]

### 食飯未啊? — have you eaten yet?
- **Jyutping:** sik6 faan6 mei6 aa3
- **Use:** A greeting as much as a question — phatic, like "how's it going." A plain
  「食咗」or 「未啊」is a complete answer; it's not usually an invitation.
- **Register:** casual, warm. Very common from older colleagues.
- **Contrast:** answering with detail about your lunch misreads it.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-01 · source: seed
