# Talking About Money

Explaining money to family — which in practice means explaining it to a parent who is
worried, where the hard part is not the vocabulary but keeping the register warm and the
sentences short. Terms are in [[banking-and-finance]].

The organising insight from the first sheet on this: **the analogy does the work the
explanation can't.** 「好似兩個銀包咁」gets further in six syllables than any amount of
correct banking vocabulary, because the problem was never that Mom lacked the word for
"balance".

## Explaining and reassuring

### 好似…咁 — it's like …
- **Jyutping:** hou2 ci5 … gam2
- **Moment:** explaining to Mom that two banks can't see each other's money
- **Use:** The analogy frame, and the most useful thing on this page. Sets up a comparison
  to something concrete: 「好似兩個銀包咁」= "it's like two wallets."
- **Register:** casual to neutral, everywhere. Especially good with elders — it replaces a
  technical explanation with a picture.
- **Contrast:** strictly, words.hk writes this gam2 as 噉 and keeps 咁 for gam3 (the degree
  word — 咁多, 得咁少). Everyday Hong Kong writing uses 咁 for both, so 咁 here is fine;
  just don't read it as gam3.
- **Example:** 好似兩個銀包咁，一個唔知另一個有幾多錢。— hou2 ci5 loeng5 go3 ngan4 baau1
  gam2, jat1 go3 m4 zi1 ling6 jat1 go3 jau5 gei2 do1 cin2. "It's like two wallets — one
  doesn't know how much is in the other." (The sheet had 唔會知道; 唔知 is tighter, and in
  an analogy you want the short version.)
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-04 · source: [[explaining-finances-to-mom-dump]]
- **See also:** [[banking-and-finance]]

### 完全冇關係 — nothing to do with each other
- **Jyutping:** jyun4 cyun4 mou5 gwaan1 hai6
- **Moment:** telling Mom the two banks are unconnected
- **Use:** Two things are unrelated. 「呢兩間銀行完全冇關係㗎」. Carries 㗎 well, because
  this is usually said to correct an assumption.
- **Register:** casual to neutral.
- **Contrast:** 獨立 (duk6 laap6) is the word a bank letter would use — "independent".
  Correct, and it makes you sound like the letter. 冇關係 is what you say to your mother.
  Note 冇關係 also means "it doesn't matter" in reply to an apology; context separates them
  completely and nobody is confused in practice.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-04 · source: [[explaining-finances-to-mom-dump]]
- **See also:** [[final-particles]], [[banking-and-finance]]

### 睇唔到 — can't see it
- **Jyutping:** tai2 m4 dou2
- **Moment:** why one bank's app doesn't show the other bank's balance
- **Use:** The verb + 唔到 pattern: unable to, despite trying. 「你喺呢間睇唔到嗰間嘅結餘」.
- **Register:** casual, universal.
- **Contrast:** 唔睇 (m4 tai2) is "doesn't look" — a choice. 睇唔到 is "can't see" — an
  inability. For a worried parent that difference is the whole reassurance: the bank isn't
  hiding it, it just can't reach it.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-04 · source: [[explaining-finances-to-mom-dump]]

### 唔使擔心 — don't worry
- **Jyutping:** m4 sai2 daam1 sam1
- **Use:** Closes a reassurance. 「銀行啲錢有政府保障，唔使擔心」.
- **Register:** warm, casual to neutral.
- **Contrast:** 唔好擔心 (m4 hou2 …) is closer to "don't you worry" — a mild instruction.
  唔使 is "there's no need", which gives a reason rather than an order, and lands better
  with a parent. Same 唔使 / 唔好 split as in [[softening-and-politeness]].
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-04 · source: [[explaining-finances-to-mom-dump]]
- **See also:** [[softening-and-politeness]]

## Money moving around

### 分開擺 — kept separately
- **Jyutping:** fan1 hoi1 baai2
- **Moment:** her money is in two places, and that's deliberate, not an accident
- **Use:** 「你啲錢分開咗擺喺兩間銀行」= "your money is split across two banks."
  擺 (baai2) is the everyday "put / keep somewhere".
- **Register:** casual.
- **Contrast:** the measure word follows the noun, not the money: 兩**間**銀行 but
  兩**個**戶口. Swapping 銀行 for 戶口 and keeping 間 is the slip to avoid.
- **Example:** 冇唔見，全部都喺度，只係分開咗擺。— mou5 m4 gin3, cyun4 bou6 dou1 hai2
  dou6, zi2 hai6 fan1 hoi1 zo2 baai2. "Nothing's lost, it's all there, just kept in
  separate places."
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-04 · source: [[explaining-finances-to-mom-dump]]
- **See also:** [[banking-and-finance]]

### 隨時都可以 — you can … anytime
- **Jyutping:** ceoi4 si4 dou1 ho2 ji5
- **Use:** The reassurance that money isn't locked up. 「啲錢隨時都可以攞出嚟」.
  The 都 is not optional — 隨時可以 alone sounds clipped.
- **Register:** casual to neutral.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-04 · source: [[explaining-finances-to-mom-dump]]

### 幫你轉賬 — I'll do the transfer for you
- **Jyutping:** bong1 nei5 zyun2 zoeng3
- **Use:** 「如果要轉錢，我可以幫你轉賬」. Note the register split inside one sentence:
  轉錢 (zyun2 cin2) for what she wants to do, 轉賬 (zyun2 zoeng3) for the banking
  operation. That's natural, not inconsistent.
- **Register:** 轉錢 casual, 轉賬 neutral/banking.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-04 · source: [[explaining-finances-to-mom-dump]]
- **See also:** [[softening-and-politeness]] (幫我… is the same frame pointed the other way)

## Her side of the conversation

### 得咁少 — only this little
- **Jyutping:** dak1 gam3 siu2
- **Moment:** Mom looking at one bank's balance and thinking money has gone
- **Use:** 得 before a quantity means "only that much", with a note of disappointment.
  「點解呢間銀行話我得咁少錢嘅?」= "why does this bank say I've only got this little?"
- **Register:** casual.
- **Contrast:** this 咁 *is* gam3 — degree, "so/such" — unlike the gam2 of 好似…咁 above.
  Same character, two words. The sentence-final 嘅 here is ge2, the changed tone that makes
  it a genuine puzzled question rather than a statement.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-04 · source: [[explaining-finances-to-mom-dump]]
- **See also:** [[final-particles]], [[tone-sandhi]]

### 唔好俾人知 — don't let anyone know
- **Jyutping:** m4 hou2 bei2 jan4 zi1
- **Use:** 「唔好俾人知你個密碼呀」. 俾人知 is literally "give people to know" — the
  ordinary way to say "let someone find out".
- **Register:** casual. The 呀 (aa3) at the end is what keeps it caring rather than
  scolding; without it this is a telling-off.
- **Status:** new · captured 2026-11-04 · source: [[explaining-finances-to-mom-dump]]
- **See also:** [[final-particles]]
