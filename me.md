# Me

Read this at the start of a session. Keep it current — it's how you know what to
emphasize. See [[CLAUDE]] for the conventions this wiki runs on.

## Level

Conversational. Holds real conversations; sounds stiff and textbook doing it.
Not a beginner — beginner material doesn't belong in this wiki.

## Work

Software engineer at a Hong Kong **precast concrete** company, started around
October 2026. Owns the e-commerce platform contractors order from. This matters for the
wiki in two ways: the daily shop talk is construction vocabulary (石屎, 沙井, 承建商 —
see [[concrete-and-construction]]), and the daily work talk is heavily code-mixed tech
Cantonese ([[software-and-tech]]). Being new is itself a conversational situation —
see [[describing-my-job]].

## Family

Explains household admin to his mother in Cantonese — banks, accounts, forms. A recurring
situation and a demanding one: the content is technical, the register has to stay warm and
short, and the formal banking vocabulary is exactly what sounds wrong with a parent. See
[[talking-about-money]]. The transferable lesson so far is that an analogy beats an
explanation — 「好似兩個銀包咁」lands where 結餘 does not.

## Goal

Speak Cantonese in daily life the way people actually speak it. The target isn't more
vocabulary, it's **the same ideas said the way a native speaker would say them.**

## What to weight

1. **Final particles** — the biggest single gap. See [[final-particles]].
2. **Register** — friends vs work vs elders vs shop staff.
3. **Collocation** — the verb that actually goes with the noun.
4. **Stiff → natural contrasts** — for anything he already knows a formal version of.
5. **Slang and HK code-switching**, English included.

## Weak spots

_To be filled in as they show up. Candidates to watch for:_

- Reaching for written/Mandarin-flavoured forms in speech (請, 完成, 討論, 下班).
- Dropping particles, leaving sentences grammatical but blunt.
- Over-careful pronunciation — see [[lazy-sounds]].
- **Dictionary tones where the spoken form has a changed tone.** The source he brought
  in on 2026-11-04 had 軟件 as jyun5 gin6 (should be gin6-2), 訂單 as ding6 daan1 (should
  be deng6), 生產 as saang1 caan2 (should be sang1) — all three the literary/dictionary
  reading in place of the spoken one. Watch whether this repeats; see [[tone-sandhi]].
